# How `fix` is paid in chat

`fix` uses the Payment Protocol (seller role) when `FET_PAY_TO`, `FET_PRICE` and `FET_REFUND_CONTACT` are set. First the lead scans the page for free, holding a job slot only for that scan; if the agent is busy, the scan errors, the page has no issues or no patch could cover them, it replies in chat and sends no payment request. Otherwise the lead sends a `RequestPayment` for `FET_PRICE` plus a small amount unique to the job (under 0.001 FET), in real FET (`fet_direct`), to `FET_PAY_TO`, the owner's wallet, with the job id as `reference` and a 300 s deadline. The buyer pays on Fetch mainnet **either that exact amount or at least the price with the tx memo set to the reference**, and sends `CommitPayment` with the tx hash and that reference. The lead checks the tx on chain (successful, a bank send to that wallet in `afet`, bound to this job by the exact amount or the memo, hash never used before). A real payment with neither binding logs `REFUND_DUE` once and gets the refund contact. The payment path has been tested with a stand-in chain, not yet with a real ASI:One wallet. Only then does it run the fix, and it replies `CompletePayment` after the report is delivered. A tx that is not indexed yet gets "transaction not found yet - resend CommitPayment". Any other mismatch gets a "Payment not accepted: <reason>" chat line. In both cases the reference stays open until its deadline. A reference is consumed once, by a verified payment, and only the sender who asked can use it. `CancelPayment` is sent only when a reference closes unpaid: at the deadline or after `RejectPayment`. The buyer pays before the job runs, so if the job fails or confirms no fix, the reply gives the refund contact and the server logs `REFUND_DUE` with the job and tx; refunds are made by hand. State is in memory, so a job paid just before a restart is lost and refunded by hand. If these settings are missing or invalid, chat `fix` replies with the x402 terms only.
