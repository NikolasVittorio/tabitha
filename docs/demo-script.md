# Demo scripts

Wording rules: never claim the page meets a standard. The keyboard line reads "Keyboard test: N focus stops walked, M keyboard issues." and the honest line reads "Not tested: N criteria" (N and M come from the actual run; do not invent numbers). Screen-reader audio is pre-recorded with NVDA, never live. Fixes are paid in real money: real FET through the Payment Protocol, or USDC on Base over x402. Show a real payment only if one has actually settled; otherwise label it as a test.

Demo page: `demo/broken.html` at a public address you control, so you can apply the changes and recheck. Demo line: "Before: 'button. button. image.' After: 'Buy now, button. image.'"

## Walkthrough (3 to 5 minutes)

1. **Problem and who it is for (0:00-0:30).** Agents ship pages at machine speed; unlabelled buttons, missing alt text, low contrast and keyboard traps reach screen reader and keyboard users. Expected callers are builder agents; people can chat with it too.
2. **Architecture (0:30-1:15).** One uAgents Bureau, four agents: lead (Chat and Payment Protocol, mailbox), scanner (axe-core 4.14.0 in Playwright), walker (keyboard walk, up to 50 Tab presses), fixer (rules only, no LLM). Show the four addresses.
3. **Step 1: broken page (1:15-1:45).** Open `demo/broken.html`. [AUDIO: before]
4. **Step 2: keyboard walk (1:45-2:15).** Run `check <url>`. Show the reply line: Keyboard test: N focus stops walked, M keyboard issues. Mention logical order and obscured focus are reported as needing review, never as failures.
5. **Step 3: patch (2:15-3:00).** Run `python -m agents.demo_buyer <url>`. Show the `RequestPayment` with its exact amount, the `CommitPayment` and the `CompletePayment`. Mention agents can also pay USDC over x402 at `https://api.nik0.io/fix`. Then **Changes to make**, **Needs you**, and "Not tested: N criteria". Show one change marked "verify wording", one AI suggestion, and one image that needs alt text. Apply the changes to the page and send `recheck` to show the count drop.
6. **Step 4: audio after (3:00-3:20).** [AUDIO: after] Point at the "What an AI agent sees, before and after the fixes" section of the reply (Before and After trees).
7. **Step 5: receipt and payment (3:20-4:00).** Show the Automated Scan Log, verify the signature against the key published in the README, show the Fetch mainnet tx and the reference. State it is valid for that exact input at that time only.
8. **Honest limits (4:00-4:30).** Show the disclaimer and the untested criteria. Automated coverage is roughly 40% at best (the GOV.UK GDS audit's best tool). Show `check http://169.254.169.254/` refused, and `check https://example.com` returning no automated violations with the same disclaimer.
9. **Close (4:30-4:45).** Address, repo, badges, how a builder agent calls us.
